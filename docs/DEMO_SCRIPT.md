# Demo script

The click path for the live demonstration (about 100 seconds of the 6-minute talk) on https://fitness-coach-psi-olive.vercel.app. Mohammad drives. It follows `docs/PRESENTATION.md` (Live demo) with two differences: the coach step is a chat exchange (no screen asks the model for a plan yet, D12), and the photo step comes last. The timing follows the 22 November slide plan: the demo runs 3:00 to 4:40 and is cut at 4:40 (the older 1:30 to 4:00 slot in `PRESENTATION.md` is superseded). The Hevy import (PR #147) is not on main at the time of writing and stays out unless it merges by Thu 26 Nov. "Why this plan" is not mounted on any screen on main, so it is not shown.

Status: written 23 Nov from the code on main (3d6bad7) and the presentation plan. The timings column is filled from the rehearsal walk on the live site.

## Before the demo (once, the day before and again 10 minutes before)

- One demo account, verified, onboarded, with an active plan, two weeks of logged workouts and one progress photo. It must have no unfinished workout, or one in progress with sets left. Sign in once on the demo laptop so the session is warm, then sign out so step 1 shows a real sign-in.
- The coach needs a trial, pro or admin tier. The free trial can be started once per account (only if it never had one) and lasts 7 days from the moment it is started on `/account`, so start it on Thu 3 Dec for Fri 4 Dec (a trial started on 23 Nov has ended by then).
- Vercel settings for the coach (issue #57): `GROQ_API_KEY`, `GEMINI_API_KEY`, `COACH_DEMO_MODE=1`, `GEMINI_MODEL=gemini-3.5-flash-lite`. One real chat exchange on the live site the day before.
- Browser at 1280 x 720, zoom 100%, tabs open in step order, notifications off, display mirrored, Wi-Fi checked. The backup recording of steps 5 and 6 (coach and photo) is open in a separate window.

## Rules on stage

- One action per click and say what happened after it; never click twice on **Log set**.
- If a step has not answered after 3 seconds, say what it is doing ("the request is on its way to the server") and move on at 6 seconds to the fallback.
- Never open `/account`, billing or an admin view on stage.
- Steps 5 and 6 depend on the network, the model providers and the bucket, so the backup recording stays open; it supplements the live demo and never replaces it (course rule, Lecture 10).
