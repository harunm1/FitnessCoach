# Competitor teardown: JEFIT, Hevy, Google Health (Fit, Fitbit, Health Connect), JuggernautAI

Written 25 November 2026 to drive the architecture, mainly the program model ([D35](ARCHITECTURE.md)). It extends the benchmark of 10 November (`docs/design-reviews/2026-11-10-competitor-benchmark.md`), which was a survey; this one reads each app's own pages for the features we are building.

**Method.** Primary sources only: each app's help centre, feature pages, App Store listing or developer documentation. Every page was read on **25 November 2026** (the date of every source below). The apps describe themselves, so every cell is a vendor claim about what the app does, not something we tested in the app. Where a page did not say, the cell says "not stated" (which is not the same as "absent"). Reviews, third-party comparison sites and the apps' own comparison articles were not used as evidence. Screenshots were read as described in the page text; no image or page text was copied into the repo, only short facts in our own words.

**Not done.** Strong and Fitbod (optional, no time). JEFIT's and Google Health's App Store listings were not read (Hevy's and JuggernautAI's were). JuggernautAI's FAQ answers are collapsed on the page and did not load, so its equipment, example-workout and "how is it different" answers are not used. Firecrawl credits ran out mid-session; three pages were read through a plain fetch instead.

### Platform status of Google Fit and Fitbit (matters for any integration)

- The Google Fit APIs "will be deprecated in 2026", and no new developer sign-ups have been accepted since 1 May 2024 (G2, page updated 8 Oct 2026). The Health Connect page says the Fit APIs "will be supported until the end of 2026" (G4, updated 3 Feb 2026).
- The legacy Fitbit Web API and Fitbit OAuth endpoints are stated to have been turned off from 2 November 2026; developers are told to move to the Google Health API (G3). That is Google's statement; we did not call the endpoints to confirm.
- The Fitbit app is now the Google Health app (G5). Google Health Coach is a Premium feature, shown only to people with a Fitbit or Pixel Watch (G1).
- Consequence: we should not integrate with Google Fit or the Fitbit Web API. Health Connect or the Google Health API would be the only routes, and neither is in scope for the course project.

## What FitnessCoach takes

Each line names the FitnessCoach issue or task it feeds. Tasks are the files in `.claude/commands/`.

1. **Template baseline plus AI adaptation** (task `fc-template-programs-26nov`; issues #11 coach plan generation, #58 personalize with the coach, #65 RPE target per exercise). JEFIT shows that a library plan the user can edit and then have progressed is a working hybrid (J1). Hevy's Trainer is rule-based and says so (H3). We take: seeded templates, each with its own progression rule, and the model adapting a template within bounds that code checks. Weights and percentages come from code, as in D21 and the verifier; the model chooses and phrases.
2. **Last weights shown and prefilled** (task `fc-last-weights-25nov`). Both JEFIT and Hevy show last time's numbers on every set (J1, H1, H7), and both let the user choose whether "last time" means any workout or the same routine (J1, H2). We take the prefill and the "Last:" line. Hevy keeps the previous value apart from the planned value (H2); we do the same, so a prefill never overrides the plan's target.
3. **Session timer and rest alerts** (task `fc-session-timer-25nov`; issue #171 elapsed time, closed). Hevy: the rest countdown starts when a set is ticked, per exercise, plus or minus 15 s, with a sound and volume (H1, H8). JEFIT: it keeps running in the background (J1). We take those and add opt-in sound, vibration and notification.
4. **Camera capture** (task `fc-camera-capture-26nov`; issue #16 progress photos). Hevy's Overlay of the last photo for matching the pose is the one feature worth copying (H5). We take: a take-photo action with the previous photo as a ghost overlay, private by default, and one place for both progress and form photos.
5. **Post-workout debrief** (task `fc-coach-debrief-25nov`; issue #84 coach memory). Google Health Coach sends a post-workout breakdown with tips and asks for a difficulty rating that feeds later advice (G1); JEFIT ends a session with a summary and one RPE (J2). We take the report plus the rating, with every number computed in code.
6. **Units** (D30, on main). JuggernautAI added separate profile and exercise units late (A2). Our D30 stores kilograms once and converts at the edges, which already covers a user who trains in pounds and weighs in kilograms.
7. **Offline logging** (D31, issue #185). JEFIT and Hevy both promise offline logging (J1, H7); neither page says how duplicates or a lost connection are handled. D31 states an idempotent replay.
8. **Import and export** (issue #131 import from Hevy or Strong, closed; PR #184 downloads, not yet landed when this was written). Hevy and JEFIT both export a CSV (H6, J1); Hevy also imports Strong (H6 area). This confirms CSV as the shared format.

## What we deliberately do differently

- **Checked plans.** None of the four pages read says that a generated plan is checked in code before it is shown. Ours is: `verifyPlan` runs before a plan is saved (issues #11, #66), and a failed check falls back to the rule-based plan.
- **Pain and scope are enforced in code, not in a disclaimer.** Google Health Coach says not to rely on it to judge whether an exercise is safe in pain (G1); Hevy's Trainer asks users to accept a health notice (H3). We stop or lower a session from the user's pain score (issues #43, #92) and keep medical and nutrition advice out of scope since 8 November.
- **Not a per-session generator.** JEFIT criticises tools that "generate one session at a time" (J1, vendor view); we agree with the design reason: a weekly plan that is verified, then adapted (D21).
- **Not powerlifting first.** JuggernautAI is built for squat, bench and deadlift with a meet date (A1). We cover general strength plus running for a gym user.
- **Not a social or watch product.** Hevy and JEFIT have feeds, contests and watch apps (J1, H7). They are out of scope; our social surface is none, and photos stay private.
- **No wearable dependency.** The Google coach needs a Fitbit or Pixel Watch (G1), and its upstream APIs are being retired (G2, G3). FitnessCoach works from what the user types.
- **Free logging and last weights.** Both JEFIT and Hevy keep core logging free (J1, H7). We do not put the session features of this document behind a plan.

## Limits of this document

Vendor pages describe intent, and an app can differ from its page. Hevy's offline and JEFIT's offline claims are not tested. Cells marked "not stated" should not be read as "missing". Prices are as listed on 25 November 2026 and change often (the JuggernautAI figures include a Black Friday offer that ends 30 November).
