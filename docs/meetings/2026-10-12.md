## What we discussed

- **Which project.** Two options: the event management and ticketing system from the course list, or an AI fitness coach. The ticketing system has its scope defined for us; the fitness coach lets the team decide how far to build it. We went with the fitness coach.
- **Features to scope in the proposal**, none confirmed yet: an AI coach (one advanced feature), workout tracking, progress photos in cloud storage, authentication and access control, external APIs, a free trial with Stripe in test mode, and real-time chat with the coach. The course requires at least two advanced features, so the AI coach plus one more is the minimum; a third only if time allows.
- **Form feedback** comes from what the user tells the coach in chat, not a camera, which keeps video processing out of scope.
- **Stack in the first draft:** Next.js full stack with TypeScript, Tailwind and shadcn/ui; PostgreSQL with Prisma; DigitalOcean Spaces for photos; Gemini free tier with Groq as backup. To be settled in the proposal (see `docs/RESEARCH.md`).

## Decisions

1. **Project:** an AI fitness coach web application. A user takes a quiz on goals, habits and where they train, gets a workout plan, and an AI coach explains each exercise and keeps them on track.
2. **Repository:** `harunm1/FitnessCoach`, public, so TAs need no access. Everyone commits their own work so the history shows each person's contribution.
3. **Workflow:** one branch per change, a teammate tests and reviews it, then merge. The proposal is drafted in a shared Google Doc and moves into the repo once settled.
4. **Communication:** coordination on Discord; finished proposal sections are posted there for the others to read.
